1. **Admission photo নয়:** public বা internal কোনো admission form photo নেবে না; `AdmissionApplication`/enrolment photo migration বা transfer নেই।

4. **Privacy (owner confirmed 2026-10-07):** Student photos private থাকবে। Public base URL/ACL/shared-cache, unsigned URLs বা unprotected production filesystem media অনুমোদিত নয়।
