How the numbers on this page are made
  • — = no mark entered yet. Left out of total, %, GPA and pass/fail here.
  • × = this student does not sit that paper (optional not chosen, other religion). Never counted, never a fail.
  • F = a real entered mark below the pass mark. A real 0 is a fail too — only an empty cell is pending.
  • Position is withheld while a student has a pending subject: ranking them on 8 papers against a class that sat 10 would be wrong.
  • † = provisional figure: percentage, GPA and grade are computed only on the papers entered so far, and the N/M papers line under the status says how many that is.
  • The official result pages are unchanged: a published result still grades a blank assigned subject as F ({{ absent_subject_fails|yesno:"EXAM_ABSENT_SUBJECT_FAILS is ON,EXAM_ABSENT_SUBJECT_FAILS is OFF" }}).
